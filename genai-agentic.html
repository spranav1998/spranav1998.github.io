Production principles

Control the action boundary

Human approval, ambiguity handling and role checks sit before consequential tool calls such as communication, scheduling and provisioning.

Evaluate the workflow

Model quality is only one layer. I monitor routing, tool success, retrieval, safety, latency and cost across representative scenarios.

Design for recovery

Persisted state, traceable steps, background execution and cloud observability help agents resume safely and make failures debuggable.
